  REST/GraphQL query, scraping, clone/fetch/pull, diff/patch/log/artifact read,
  or repository discovery/search.

  GitHub-read descriptors, or public provider-loop enablement.

  repository content, patch, or chain-of-thought persistence.
- No automatic investigation/recovery, retry, approval revocation, or changes
  to `resume_supported=false` or `provider_tool_loop_enabled=false`.
